LIVE Studio for Linux

unofficial

Unofficial and unsupported. Not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by TikTok or ByteDance. It talks to TikTok the way the official LIVE Studio app does, which may break at any time or go against TikTok's terms. Use it at your own risk.

Account

Log in with your TikTok account. The login page is TikTok's own; this app never sees your password, only the session cookie TikTok sets afterwards.

OBS

Set up adds a "TikTok Vertical" profile (1080x1920, NVENC) and scene collection to OBS without changing your existing ones. GNOME asks which screen to share, and the webcam turns on.

The robot replaces your webcam in the Camera scenes and talks when your mic does. Your webcam isn't shown while it's on.

In OBS: Tools, then WebSocket Server Settings. Enable the server and copy the password here.

Pinned window

Opens any web page in its own window that you can keep on top of your game.

To keep it on top: click the pinned window, press Alt+Space, and choose Always on Top. If a fullscreen game still covers it, switch the game to borderless or windowed fullscreen.
